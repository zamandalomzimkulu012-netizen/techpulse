How to make your phone faster

A phone can slow down when storage is nearly full, too many apps run in the background, or software becomes outdated.

  1. Remove apps you no longer use.
  2. Free up storage by deleting unnecessary downloads and duplicate media.
  3. Install available system and app updates.
  4. Restart your phone regularly.
  5. Be careful with apps that promise unrealistic “one-click” performance boosts.

What is artificial intelligence?

Artificial intelligence, or AI, refers to computer systems designed to perform tasks that normally require human-like abilities, such as recognizing patterns, generating text, translating languages or analyzing information.

AI is now used in search, smartphones, customer service, education, creative tools and many other areas.

Basic online security

Use unique passwords, turn on two-factor authentication where available, keep your devices updated and avoid entering sensitive information on suspicious websites.

Never share one-time passwords or verification codes with someone who contacts you unexpectedly.
